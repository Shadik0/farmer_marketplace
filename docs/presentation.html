02 / Problem Statement

The Middleman Crisis in Produce Supply

Key Real-World Challenges

  • Intermediary Gouging: 3 to 5 middlemen take up to 45% of consumer spend.
  • Zero Availability Transparency: Farmers struggle to announce real-time harvest quantities and unit prices.
  • Inventory Mismanagement: Risk of overselling leading to cancelled orders and wasted logistics.
  • Lack of Consumer Trust: Buyers have no visibility into harvest dates or authentic farm provenance.

Target User Groups

  • 👨‍🌾 Smallholder Farmers: Looking to publish available crops and receive direct payments without commission loss.
  • 👩‍🍳 Households & Chefs: Demanding fresh, verified harvest produce delivered or picked up directly.
  • 🏢 Wholesalers & Grocers: Requiring bulk tier discounts and transparent farm contracts.

07 / Backend Implementation

Business Rules & Transaction Integrity

1. Atomic Stock Deduction

When an order is requested, the backend validates available stock in a single transaction. If requested quantity exceeds stock, an HTTP 400 error is raised with a clear message.

2. Stock Restoration on Cancel

When an order transitions to cancelled, an automatic query credits all line item quantities back to the product's available stock pool immediately.

3. Duplicate Produce Detection

The listing engine detects if a farmer creates multiple listings with identical titles, automatically setting is_duplicate = 1 and routing them to Admin moderation.

4. Prototype Safety Considerations

Simulated payment modes (Cash on Pickup, COD, Demo UPI). Real banking credentials and cards are strictly never collected.
